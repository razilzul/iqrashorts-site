Privacy

IqraShorts by Rzee Studios, Singapore · Last updated 19 November 2026

IqraShorts is a free app with short cards of Quran, hadith and doa. It has no account, no ads and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Everything personal stays on your phone and is never sent to us: the cards you save, your reactions, the topics you mute, your language, how long you read each card, and the feed preferences the app learns from that. Deleting the app deletes all of it. You can also reset it in Settings.

What the app asks the internet for

  • Translations of the Quran. When an ayah is on screen, the app asks Quran Foundation's service for its translation. The request contains only the verse number and the translation chosen. Like any website, that service sees your device's internet address. The translation is kept on your phone for at most 6 days.
  • Nothing else is sent today. If we add a feature that sends something (for example a button to report a card), we will update this page before that version is released.

Sharing

When you share a card, the app makes an image on your phone and hands it to the app you pick. We never see what you share or with whom.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Questions or a request: privacy@iqrashorts.app.
