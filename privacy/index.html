Privacy

IqraShorts by Rzee Studios, Singapore · Last updated 19 November 2026 (before release)

IqraShorts is a free app with short cards of Quran, hadith and doa. It has no account, no ads and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Everything personal stays on your phone and is never sent to us: the cards you save, your likes, the topics you mute, your language, how long each card stayed on screen, and the feed preferences the app learns from that. It is excluded from Android's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, and on iPhone it is kept out of iCloud backup, so it is not copied off the phone. Deleting the app deletes all of it. You can also reset it in Settings.

What the app asks the internet for

  • Translations of the Quran. When a card with an ayah is being prepared (which can be just before it reaches the screen), the app asks our small server for that ayah's translation. Our server passes the request on to Quran Foundation's service and stores nothing. The request contains only the verse number and the chosen translation. Quran Foundation sees our server's request, not your phone. Each translation is kept on your phone for at most 6 days and is deleted when the app next reads it, starts, or returns to the screen after that.
  • A short list of cards under review. When the app starts, and when you come back to it, it downloads a list of cards that have been reported and are shown less or hidden while we check them. The request carries nothing about you.
  • Reports, only if you send one. If you tap "Report a problem" on a card, the app sends exactly three things: which card, the reason you picked from a fixed list, and a random install number the app made on your phone. The install number isn't linked to your name, email, account or location; it only lets us limit repeat reports, which means reports sent from the same phone can be told apart from others. Our server also records when the report arrived. Reports are deleted after one year. They are stored in our own section of a database we run for our other app, HalalSnap, kept separate from that app's data.
  • Links you tap. "Read free at…" and other links open in your browser, which then talks to that website directly.
  • Official prayer timetables. If you set up prayer times in Singapore or Malaysia, the app downloads the official timetable from MUIS (data.gov.sg) or JAKIM e-Solat. That tells them which of the two countries' timetables you need. For Malaysia the app downloads every zone, so your zone and your GPS position are never sent. Prayer times are worked out on your phone.
  • App updates. When the app starts, it asks our update service (Expo EAS Update) whether a newer version of the app's code and content is ready, and downloads it if so. The request carries the app version, the platform, which update you're on and a random number that the update system makes for this install. If the app failed to start last time, it also sends the error message and which update failed, so a broken update can be stopped. Expo keeps its own service logs.
  • Your internet address. Like any website, each service the app talks to sees your device's internet address: our hosting provider (Supabase) for translations, the review list and reports; Expo for update checks; and MUIS (data.gov.sg) or JAKIM for prayer timetables. Supabase's logs keep, for a short time, the address, a rough country worked out from it and what was asked for (for example which verse's translation). We don't store the address with reports and don't use it to identify you.
  • Nothing else is sent. If we add a feature that sends something more, we will update this page before that version is released.

Sharing

When you share a card, the app makes an image on your phone and hands it to the app you pick. We never see what you share or with whom.

Deleting your data

On your phone: in the app, open Settings and tap "Reset my feed and saves", or delete the app. That removes everything the app keeps on the phone.

Reports you sent: they are deleted automatically one year after they arrive. To have them deleted sooner, email privacy@iqrashorts.app with the card(s) and the date you reported them. We delete the matching reports within 30 days. Reports aren't linked to your name or email, so we can't look them up any other way.

Server logs (internet address, rough country, what was asked for) are short-lived and deleted on our hosting provider's normal schedule.

Children

The app has no accounts and collects nothing about who you are, from anyone, including children. Apart from what is listed above (requests for translations, the review list, timetables and update checks, and reports you choose to send), nothing is sent.
